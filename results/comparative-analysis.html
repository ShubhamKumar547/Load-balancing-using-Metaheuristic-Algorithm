How to interpret the results

  • Makespan: time when the last cloudlet finishes.
  • Response time: interpret according to the calculation implemented in your simulation.
  • Load imbalance: lower values indicate a more balanced distribution under the metric used by your code.
  • Throughput: completed cloudlets per unit of simulation time.
  • Compare algorithms only within the same scenario and workload.
  • These are descriptive averages; multiple runs and variability statistics are needed for stronger research conclusions.
